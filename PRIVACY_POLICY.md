# PowerFlow Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 23, 2025**

## Overview
PowerFlow ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our Power BI custom visual.

## Information We Collect
We do not collect, store, or process any personal information through the PowerFlow visual. The visual operates entirely within Power BI and processes only the data you provide within your Power BI reports.

## Data Usage
- All data processing occurs locally within your Power BI environment
- No data is transmitted to external servers or third-party services
- The visual only displays and visualizes the organizational data you import into Power BI

## Third-Party Services
PowerFlow uses the following third-party libraries:
- D3.js (Data-Driven Documents) - Open source visualization library
- Power BI Custom Visuals API - Microsoft's official API for custom visuals

## Security
We are committed to ensuring the security of your information. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
